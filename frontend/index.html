Driven By Compassion, Backed By Structure

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla, the InAmigos Foundation operates as a registered non-profit network dedicated to establishing long-term, structural welfare solutions across local communities.

Our operational model bridges strategic development and on-the-ground volunteer frameworks, ensuring that direct public support reaches target populations efficiently.

Our Impact Frameworks

Explore the six dedicated initiatives fueling sustainable social transformation and active volunteer contributions across our networks.

Humanitarian Aid

Project Sewa

Serving humanity through systematic food distribution channels, essential clothing drives, and disaster relief management for marginalized individuals.

🎯 Impact Focus: Mitigating immediate nutritional and resource gaps.
Education Wing

Project BachpanSala

Nurturing young minds through fundamental foundational literacy programs, supplying study materials, and constructing basic digital learning centers.

🎯 Impact Focus: Preventing early primary educational drop-outs.
Animal Welfare

Project Jeev

Providing direct response medical intervention, rescue support, and structured population feeding drives for injured or vulnerable stray animals.

🎯 Impact Focus: Spreading empathy and reducing urban animal suffering.
Empowerment

Project Udaan

Driving digital literacy, financial education, vocational tailoring, and craft skills for women in rural locations to achieve financial self-reliance.

🎯 Impact Focus: Creating localized micro-enterprises.
Sustainability

Project Prakriti

Protecting baseline ecosystems via intensive public tree plantation drives, local public cleanup campaigns, and practical sustainability education.

🎯 Impact Focus: Expanding urban green canopies and waste reduction.
Development

Project Vikas

Enhancing youth career development and professional readiness through technical training sprints and project management experience.

🎯 Impact Focus: Equipping student networks with technical skillsets.
